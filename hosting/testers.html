Become a tester.

Testers help verify sign-in, daily delivery, the Grimoire, notifications, themes, subscriptions, restoration, and account deletion before public launch.

Join the tester group Open the Play opt-in

Available to closed testers now; join the tester group first. The Play listing and opt-in work once the closed-test release is live.

Three steps

Join, opt in, then install.

Use the same Google account you use for Google Play on your Android phone.

  1. 1
    Join the tester group
    Anyone can join without approval at groups.google.com/g/wiccan-testers.
  2. 2
    Open the Play opt-in
    After you join the group, open the official Play opt-in link. It works once the closed-test release is live.
  3. 3
    Install Wiccan and report issues
    Test on your normal device and send clear feedback through the support address.
