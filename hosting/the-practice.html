How Wiccan works

The practice.

Wiccan is not an endless feed. Each day brings one selected lesson, spell, or potion to read, practice if you choose, and save to your Grimoire.

Daily delivery

How lessons work.

A lesson, spell, or potion arrives as a full, step-by-step practice: context, materials, method, and safety notes. You can always choose reflection instead of a physical practice. Entries already delivered stay in your Grimoire.

Where the work comes from

Historical grimoires and magical texts.

Published lessons, spells, and potions are drawn from historical grimoires and ancient magical texts—such as the Key of Solomon, Agrippa, and the London-Leiden papyrus—and cited where available. Language in the app distinguishes source evidence, traditional claims, and modern reflection.

Before anything goes live

Reviewed under the owner’s authorization.

AI-assisted drafts are published after an automated and AI review under the owner’s authorization. That review checks that each lesson is a full step-by-step practice correctly cited to historical grimoires and magical texts, plus safety, rights, and originality checks.
