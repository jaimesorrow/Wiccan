Content & Safety

Effective November 7, 2026

Wiccan treats historical spiritual material as education and reflection. This guidance applies to every lesson, spell, potion, and optional practice in the app.

No promised outcomes

Wiccan does not promise or guarantee that any spell, potion, ritual, ceremony, or other practice will produce any supernatural, physical, emotional, financial, relational, or other result. Practices are presented as history and as prompts for reflection; descriptions of what a practice was meant to do report historical or traditional claims, not facts. The Terms of Service contain the full disclaimer.

No professional or emergency advice

Content is not medical, mental-health, legal, financial, veterinary, or emergency advice. It is not a diagnosis or treatment. Seek an appropriate qualified professional, and contact local emergency services or poison control when needed.

Materials, fire, and physical safety

Never ingest, inhale, apply, forage, mix, or burn a substance unless you have independently confirmed that it is safe for the intended use. Avoid unknown plants, toxic minerals, bodily fluids, unsafe smoke, and hazardous reactions.

Use flame-safe surfaces, ventilation, supervision, and local fire rules. Keep materials away from children and animals. Substitute a written or visual reflection whenever physical practice creates risk.

Consent, law, and respect

Do not involve another person, their property, remains, protected sites, wildlife, or restricted cultural material without permission and lawful authority. Respect living traditions and do not present a modern adaptation as an authentic reconstruction.

Editorial standard

AI-assisted drafts can only use vetted ancient-grimoire source records. A human must review source grounding, duplication, safety, rights, and wording before publication.

Report a safety, rights, or accuracy concern through Contact Support. Content may be corrected or withdrawn while it is reviewed.
