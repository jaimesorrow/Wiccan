Potion Safety

Effective November 16, 2026

Potion, infusion, bath, oil, spray, jar, tea, and skin-contact practices can involve real physical risks. This guidance supplements each lesson's specific safety note. If a lesson is unclear, an ingredient is unknown, or you cannot use it safely, do not make or use the preparation.

Ritual use and ingestion

Unless a lesson clearly identifies a preparation as ordinary food or drink made only with food-grade ingredients and normal kitchen methods, treat it as ritual use only and do not consume it.

Never ingest essential oils. Do not add essential oils, fragrance oils, craft herbs, crystals, unknown plants, non-food colorants, or ritual materials to food or drink.

A spiritual label such as potion, elixir, tea, or infusion does not establish that something is safe to swallow, inhale, apply to skin, or give to another person or animal.

Ingredients, allergies, and health

Identify every ingredient before use and follow its manufacturer label. Do not use unknown, spoiled, foraged, contaminated, recalled, or hazardous substances.

Consider allergies, asthma, skin sensitivity, pregnancy or nursing, medications, medical conditions, and possible ingredient interactions. Ask an appropriate licensed health professional or pharmacist when you are unsure. Wiccan is not medical advice and no preparation is a diagnosis or treatment.

For skin contact, use only skin-safe ingredients at appropriate dilution and patch test a small area first. Stop immediately if irritation, pain, dizziness, breathing difficulty, or another adverse reaction occurs.

Mixing, heating, bathing, and ventilation

Do not combine household cleaners, bleach, acids, bases, solvents, alcohol, medicines, or reactive materials. Never heat a sealed container or use a container that is not intended for the temperature and ingredients involved.

Keep flames and hot liquids on stable heat-safe surfaces, provide ventilation, tie back loose clothing and hair, and never leave heat or flame unattended.

Keep preparations out of eyes, mouth, mucous membranes, broken skin, and other sensitive areas. For baths, use a safe water temperature, prevent slips, and leave the bath if you feel faint, irritated, or unwell.

Children, animals, and consent

Keep ingredients and finished preparations secured away from children and animals. Many plants, oils, foods, minerals, fumes, and topical products that adults tolerate can harm animals or children.

Do not give, apply, spray, burn near, or otherwise expose another person or animal to a preparation without informed consent and confirmation that the intended use is safe.

Labeling, storage, spoilage, and disposal

Label every container with its ingredients, preparation date, intended use, and “Ritual use only — do not consume” when it is not food. Store it separately from food, drinks, medicines, and personal-care products.

Use clean equipment and suitable containers. Discard a preparation if its identity, age, storage history, or safety is uncertain, or if its color, odor, texture, pressure, or container changes unexpectedly.

Follow ingredient labels and local requirements for disposal. Do not pour oils, salts, concentrated herbs, chemicals, glitter, wax, or unknown mixtures into drains, soil, or natural water.

Poisoning, injury, and emergencies

If someone may have swallowed, inhaled, or been exposed to something harmful, stop the practice and contact local emergency services or poison control. In the United States, Poison Control is available at 1-800-222-1222 and poison.org. Do not wait for symptoms or rely on a ritual response.

Bring or photograph the product label or ingredient list for responders when it is safe to do so.

Lesson-specific warnings and results

Always follow the lesson's activity-specific safety note and shared potion disclosure. This page does not override a stricter warning. If instructions conflict, choose the safer option or do not attempt the practice.

Wiccan does not promise or guarantee that any potion or related practice will produce a magical, spiritual, medical, psychological, fertility, relationship, financial, protective, or other result.
