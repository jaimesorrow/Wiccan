Delete your Wiccan account

Wiccan · developer: Wiccan App Inc.

You can delete your Wiccan account and its data at any time, with or without the app installed.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Wiccan and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

Option 2: without the app

  1. Email com.wiccan.app@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to Wiccan.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Wiccan account.
  3. We verify that the request came from the account's email address, delete the account, and reply to confirm within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address, name, Firebase user ID)
  • Your profile and policy-acceptance records
  • Your daily content delivery history and usage reservations
  • Registered devices and push-notification tokens
  • Your subscription record and stored purchase tokens

What is kept, and for how long

  • Pseudonymous purchase-token and account hashes, kept only for as long as needed to stop a Google Play purchase being reused on another account. They are not the raw purchase token or an account profile.
  • Firebase Analytics event data (up to 14 months) and Crashlytics crash reports (about 90 days), which expire on their own schedule.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, or need for security, fraud, or an open dispute, only as long as necessary.

Your subscription

Deleting your account first tries to cancel an active Google Play subscription. Deletion does not itself issue a refund; refunds follow Google Play's rules. You can also cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
