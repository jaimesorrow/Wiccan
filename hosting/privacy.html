Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Version 4

This Privacy Policy explains what information Wiccan App Inc. (“Wiccan,” “we,” or “us”) handles when you use the Wiccan Android app, why it is used, and the choices available to you. Wiccan does not sell personal data and does not show advertising.

Information handled

Account data: email address, first and last name, Firebase user ID, sign-in provider, and policy-acceptance versions and timestamps.

Service activity: delivered content IDs, dates, source metadata, daily-use reservations, time zone, notification preference, and registered push token.

Purchase data: product ID, entitlement and renewal state, expiry, purchase token, and pseudonymous account/token hashes used to prevent purchase fraud and replay.

Diagnostics and analytics: app interactions, device/app information, crash reports, and integrity signals handled through Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check, and Play Integrity. Where your device allows it, Firebase Analytics may also collect the Android advertising ID; Wiccan uses it only for analytics measurement, never for advertising. You can reset or delete it in your device's Google or privacy settings.

The app does not request precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health permissions. A device time-zone identifier is not used to infer precise location.

Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, avoid repeating daily content, show archive history, deliver requested notifications, verify subscription access, prevent fraud, enforce security, diagnose failures, understand app performance, and meet legal or platform obligations.

Processors and disclosure

Google services process data on Wiccan's behalf or under their own terms: Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check, Play Integrity, and Google Play Billing.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or during a lawful business transfer. Wiccan does not sell personal data or share it for third-party behavioral advertising.

Retention and deletion

Account and service data is kept while your account exists and is needed for the purposes above. Event-level Firebase Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months, and Crashlytics crash reports for about 90 days.

Delete Account in Settings first attempts to cancel active Play auto-renewal, then deletes the Firebase account, profile, delivery history, usage reservations, registered devices, and subscription record. If cancellation cannot be confirmed, deletion stops so it can be retried safely.

Limited pseudonymous purchase-token and account hashes may remain for fraud and anti-replay protection, for as long as needed to stop a purchase being reused on another account. They are not the raw purchase token or an active account profile.

You may also request deletion without the app at https://wiccan-android.web.app/delete-account. Legitimate legal, security, dispute, or fraud records may be retained only as necessary.

Security and international processing

Wiccan uses transport encryption, Firebase security rules, server-authoritative writes, App Check, Play Integrity, and restricted administrator workflows. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Google services may process information in countries outside your own, subject to their safeguards and applicable transfer requirements.

Children

Wiccan is intended only for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, email com.wiccan.app@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, you may have the right to know, access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of its sale, sharing for targeted advertising, or profiling. Wiccan does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for profiling with legal or similarly significant effects.

To make a request, email com.wiccan.app@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will verify the request against your account and respond within 45 days. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our response. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Your choices and contact

You can disable daily notifications, sign out, manage your subscription in Google Play, and delete your account in Settings.

Wiccan is operated by Wiccan App Inc.. For access, correction, privacy, or deletion questions, email com.wiccan.app@gmail.com or use Contact Support in Settings.

When this policy changes materially, it receives a new version and the app asks you to review it before continuing.
