Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026 · Version 7

This Privacy Policy explains what information Jaime Sorrow (“Wiccan,” “we,” or “us”) handles when you use the Wiccan Android app and its related services, why it is used, which service providers process it, how long it is kept, and the choices available to you. Wiccan does not sell personal data. If you do not have an active subscription, the app shows ads from Google AdMob; subscribers (monthly or yearly, including during a free trial) see no ads.

Who we are

Wiccan is developed and operated by Jaime Sorrow, an individual developer based in Alaska, United States, who is responsible for (the “controller” of) your personal information. For any privacy question or request, email hello@wiccan.app or use Contact Support in Settings.

Information handled

Account data: email address, first and last name, Firebase user ID, sign-in provider, email-verification status, and policy-acceptance versions and timestamps. If you sign up with an email address, your password is stored only in hashed form by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to Wiccan. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your Google account's name, email address, and profile-picture link with Firebase Authentication; Wiccan does not display or otherwise use the profile picture.

Service activity: delivered content IDs, dates, source metadata, daily-use reservations, records of when the content library runs low for your account, time zone, notification preference, registered push token, and the time of your most recent verification-email request (used to limit resends).

Purchase data: product ID, entitlement and renewal state, expiry, purchase token, and pseudonymous account/token hashes used to prevent purchase fraud and replay. Google Play processes your payment; Wiccan never receives your card or other payment details, and it gives Google Play only a one-way hashed account identifier, never your raw user ID.

Security signals: App Check and Play Integrity attestations, used on every request to protect the service from fraud and abuse. When the app connects, Google's servers also receive your device's IP address, which Firebase Authentication and Cloud Functions use to process requests and protect sign-in from abuse. If a request fails, the server records your user ID and the error in Google Cloud Logging so the problem can be investigated.

Optional analytics and crash reports: app interactions and device/app information through Firebase Analytics, and crash reports through Crashlytics, are collected only if you opt in during onboarding or in Settings, and you can turn them off at any time. App interactions include events such as completing sign-up, viewing the day's content (with its type and tier), viewing the subscription screen, starting or losing a subscription, and deleting an account. Device/app information includes the device model, operating-system and app versions, and Firebase installation identifiers. Firebase Analytics also records screen views, sessions, and Google Play subscription events (product and price), and derives an approximate location, such as country or city, from a masked IP address. Crash reports include the stack trace and the app's state at the time of the crash. While analytics is on and your device allows it, Firebase Analytics may also collect the Android advertising ID; Wiccan uses it only for analytics measurement, never for advertising, and tells Google that ad storage, ad user data, and ad personalization are denied. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device's Google or privacy settings.

Ads for non-subscribers (Google AdMob): if you do not have an active subscription, the Grimoire screen shows one banner ad from Google AdMob. No ads appear in lessons or rituals, on the subscription screen, in Settings, during account deletion, on legal pages, or during email verification, and subscribers see no ads. To choose, show, and measure these ads and to prevent ad fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent tool may collect your Android advertising ID, device and app information (such as device model, operating-system and app versions, language, and screen size), ad interactions (such as ads shown and tapped), diagnostic and performance information, your consent choices, and your IP address, from which Google may derive an approximate location such as country or city. Where the law requires consent, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before any ad is requested; where U.S. state privacy laws apply, it lets you opt out of personalized ads. If you do not allow personalized ads, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which use the context of the app and limited data for frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices in Settings, where offered, and you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device's Google or privacy settings. Wiccan does not send your name, email address, or account ID to AdMob.

On your device: the app keeps today's content in a local cache and stores your theme, notification, and analytics choices. The content cache and sign-in data are excluded from Android backups; your theme, notification, and analytics choices may be included in your device's own Android backup. Uninstalling the app removes this data from the device but does not delete your account.

Support messages: if you email hello@wiccan.app, we receive your email address and whatever your message contains.

The app requests only the notification permission, for the optional daily reminder and, during a free trial, a reminder before the trial ends. You can turn either off in Android's notification settings for Wiccan. It does not request precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health permissions. A device time-zone identifier is not used to infer precise location. The app also declares Android's advertising ID permission, which shows no prompt, so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can read the advertising ID described above.

Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, send account emails (verification, password reset, and email-change confirmation), avoid repeating daily content, show archive history, deliver requested notifications, remind you before a free trial ends, verify subscription access, answer support requests, prevent fraud, enforce security, meet legal or platform obligations, show and measure ads for users without a subscription, and, only if you opt in, diagnose crashes and understand app performance.

Wiccan uses an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) on its servers only to draft lesson text from historical source passages. Lessons are then checked by automated tools and an AI assistant under the owner's authorization before publishing. No account, usage, device, or other personal information is sent to the AI provider, and the app has no feature that sends what you type to an AI service.

Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and similar laws)

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss law, or a similar law applies, Wiccan relies on these legal bases: performance of its contract with you, to create and secure your account, deliver the daily content and notifications you request, and provide your subscription; legitimate interests, to prevent fraud and abuse, keep the service secure, and show non-personalized ads to users without a subscription where the law allows, balanced against your rights; your consent, for usage analytics and crash reports, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings, and for personalized ads and storing or reading ad information on your device, which you can change at any time with Privacy choices in Settings, where offered; and legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records and responding to lawful requests.

You may object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests by emailing hello@wiccan.app.

Service providers and disclosure

Wiccan does not share personal data with third parties for their own marketing or other independent purposes, except the ad data Google AdMob handles under its own terms, as described below. It relies on these service providers, which process data on Wiccan's behalf or, where noted, under their own terms:

Google: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Firebase Installations, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check, Google Cloud Logging, and Secret Manager, for sign-in, storage, server processing, notifications, optional diagnostics, and security; and the Gmail mailbox that receives hello@wiccan.app support email.

Google Play: subscription purchases, billing, refunds, and Play Integrity checks, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Google AdMob: ads shown to users without a subscription, together with Google's consent message, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Google acts independently for some of this processing, as explained at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cloudflare Email Routing: handles mail to hello@wiccan.app.

Firebase Authentication: sends the verification, password-reset, and change-email messages.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or during a lawful business transfer. Wiccan does not sell personal data for money. If you allow personalized ads, Google AdMob may use your advertising ID and other ad data to show ads based on your interests; some U.S. state laws treat this as “sharing” for targeted advertising, and you can opt out with Privacy choices in Settings, where offered or by deleting your advertising ID.

Retention and deletion

Account and service data is kept while your account exists and is needed for the purposes above. Event-level Firebase Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months, Crashlytics crash reports for about 90 days, and server error logs for about 30 days. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and keep a record of it. Ad data collected by Google AdMob is kept by Google under its own retention policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

Delete Account in Settings first attempts to cancel active Play auto-renewal, then deletes the Firebase account, profile, delivery history, usage reservations, content-availability records, registered devices, verification-email record, and subscription record. If cancellation cannot be confirmed, deletion stops so it can be retried safely.

Limited pseudonymous purchase-token and account hashes may remain for fraud and anti-replay protection, for as long as needed to stop a purchase being reused on another account. They are not the raw purchase token or an active account profile.

You may also request deletion without the app at https://wiccan.app/delete-account.html; we verify the request and confirm deletion within 30 days. Deleted data may remain in Google's encrypted backups for a limited period until it is overwritten. Legitimate legal, security, dispute, or fraud records may be retained only as necessary.

Security and international processing

Wiccan uses transport encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all traffic, Google Cloud's encryption at rest, Firebase security rules that block the app from writing account, purchase, or delivery records directly, server-authoritative writes, App Check, Play Integrity, server credentials held in Secret Manager, and restricted administrator workflows. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Wiccan is operated from the United States, and Google services may process information in the United States and other countries outside your own. Transfers of personal data from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland rely on the safeguards in Google's data processing terms, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

Wiccan is intended only for adults (18 or older, or the age of majority where they live if higher) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, email hello@wiccan.app and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, these include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, data portability, and withdrawing any consent you gave. Residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, Canada, Brazil, Australia, and other countries may have similar rights, including the right to know what is collected and to opt out of its sale, sharing for targeted advertising, or profiling.

Wiccan does not sell personal information for money or use it for profiling with legal or similarly significant effects. Personalized ads from Google AdMob may count as sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising under some U.S. state laws; you can opt out at any time with Privacy choices in Settings, where offered or by deleting your device's advertising ID, and subscribers see no ads.

To make a request, email hello@wiccan.app from the address on your account. We will verify the request against your account and respond within 30 days. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our response. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Your choices and contact

In Settings you can disable daily notifications, turn usage analytics and crash reports on or off, change your ad privacy choices (where offered), sign out, and delete your account. Manage your subscription in Google Play; an active subscription removes all ads.

For access, correction, privacy, or deletion questions, email hello@wiccan.app or use Contact Support in Settings. Wiccan is operated by Jaime Sorrow.

Changes to this policy

The effective date at the top of this policy shows when it last changed. When this policy changes materially, it receives a new version and the app asks you to review it before continuing.
