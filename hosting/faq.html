Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Where does the content come from?

Wiccan offers practical lessons, spells, and potions drawn from historical grimoires and ancient magical texts, such as the Key of Solomon, Agrippa, and the London-Leiden papyrus, and cited where available.

Is Wiccan free?

The app is free to download. Daily content and Grimoire access require an active free trial or Wiccan Premium subscription, which Google Play bills monthly or yearly, depending on the plan you choose. Google Play shows the localized price and billing terms before purchase.

Is Wiccan intended for children?

No. Wiccan is intended only for adults age 18 and older.

Does Wiccan promise that a practice will work?

No. Wiccan presents spiritual and historical material for education and reflection and does not guarantee any supernatural, physical, emotional, financial, or other result.

How do I join the closed test?

Join the tester group, then open the Play opt-in, then install. Full steps are on the Become a Tester page.
