A practice each dawn

The moon has turned. Your next practice awaits.

No endless feed, no noise. Each day one spell, potion, or lesson rises to meet you, with its intent, its materials, its steps in order, and the words to speak when the moment comes.

Your Grimoire

A library that grows with every moon.

Nothing you receive is lost. Every rite gathers in your own Grimoire, by date or by craft: candle spells, cord and knot work, charms, crystals, and potions, ready whenever the season calls them back.

Roots you can trace

The old source, named beneath the work.

Wiccan's rites are drawn from historical texts such as the London-Leiden magical papyrus and the Metternich Stela. Where a source is used, the work and passage are named, so you may read the ancient words for yourself and see how each modern practice was shaped from them.

Moon and hour

Work when the night is ready.

Each practice tells you when to work: the full moon, a waxing crescent, a quiet evening by lamplight. Its correspondences and the words you may speak are set beside it, so the rite fits the sky above you.

What sets Wiccan apart: real historical sources, cited where available, behind every practice.
