Care before claims

Spiritual content, presented responsibly.

No promised outcomes. Ritual and folklore are presented for education and reflection, not as guarantees of supernatural or personal results.

No medical advice. Wiccan does not diagnose or treat health conditions. Material safety notes appear where extra care is needed.

Good to know

Is Wiccan free?

The app is free to download. Daily content and Grimoire access require an active free trial or Wiccan Premium subscription, which Google Play bills monthly. Google Play shows the localized price and billing terms before purchase.

Is Wiccan intended for children?

No. Wiccan is intended only for adults age 18 and older.

Does Wiccan promise that a practice will work?

No. Wiccan presents spiritual and historical material for education and reflection and does not guarantee any supernatural, physical, emotional, financial, or other result.
