## Text identity and access

The Batch 1 Maqlū rewrites use the CMAwRo record **Q002713, Maqlû Ritual Tablet**, a composite transliteration and translation based on named first-millennium BCE witnesses. The live ORACC host returned HTTP 503 during this review. An archived capture dated 2025-01-22 preserved the complete record, its witness list, line numbers, transliteration, and English rendering:

- Live record: <https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/cmawro/Q002713>
- Inspected archive: <https://web.archive.org/web/20250122073307id_/https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu//cmawro/Q002713>

The archive is evidence of what the ORACC page displayed; it is not an independent grant of rights.

- Live licensing URL: <http://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/doc/about/licensing/>
