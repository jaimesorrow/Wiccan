# Source evidence review for the five staged lessons

Status: research evidence only. This report does not approve a passage, populate `functions/sourcePassages.json`, or authorize a Firestore write. Each candidate still needs the independent passage, fidelity, scope, rights, and safety decisions required by the migration validator.

## Edition and manuscript identity

All five staged candidates cite F. Ll. Griffith and Herbert Thompson, *The Demotic Magical Papyrus of London and Leiden*, vol. I (London: H. Grevel & Co., 1904). A scan of that edition contributed by the University of Toronto is freely readable at [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/demoticmagicalpa01grifuoft). The [University of Pennsylvania Online Books record](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupid?key=olbp18345) independently identifies Griffith and Thompson as editors and links all three original volumes.

The source is a divided manuscript, P. BM EA 10070 / P. Leiden I 383, rather than a work originally titled as a modern book. The British Museum catalogue identifies [EA 10070,1](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA10070-1) as recto columns 1–3 and [EA 10070,2](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA10070-2) as recto columns 4–7. The latter catalogue record dates the manuscript to the Roman period, 200–225 CE; Griffith and Thompson's preface describes it as third century CE. This supports the staged lessons' broad “early third century CE” dating.

## Reuse basis

The 1904 edition text is public domain in the United States because it was published well before the current US public-domain cutoff. The same Online Books record identifies Griffith's death in 1934 and Thompson's in 1944; the text is consequently also beyond a life-plus-70 term. This finding concerns the 1904 edition and translation. It does not grant rights in a later translation, editorial apparatus, website transcription, photograph, or museum image.

Use the 1904 text only as editorial evidence and write the lessons in original language. Link to the Internet Archive scan rather than copying scan images into the product. The Attalus HTML transcription is convenient for reading, but its host terms are not the rights basis for this project. Product counsel should still identify the actual distribution jurisdictions before a passage is marked rights-approved.

## Outstanding uncertainties

- The Internet Archive scan is sufficient to inspect the 1904 edition, but host access is not itself a copyright license. Rights approval should cite the public-domain legal basis by jurisdiction and avoid relying on the availability of a scan.
- Griffith and Thompson sometimes signal uncertain readings with question marks. A lesson must preserve those uncertainties rather than turn them into definite material identities or ritual meanings.
- Candidates 1–4 retain a documented sequence or construction after removing unsafe components, but the scale of adaptation still requires a human fidelity and scope decision.
- Candidate 5 has both a citation error and the weakest continuity between the attested medical purpose and proposed modern purpose. It must not enter the approved passage register in its current form.
